# DrawLab v0.3.0-alpha.1 — exact moments and sampling mathematics

Laboratory prerelease; not a verified national lottery implementation, prediction, prize calculation, or NIST-certified random generator.

## Client-visible demonstration

Choose the 5/36 laboratory model. The main-match table remains exact; its moment panel reads E[X] = 25 / 36 and Var(X) = 24025 / 45360. Expand “Why uniform sampling and what does the research establish?” to inspect the combinatorial derivation and the limitations. Change the profile to 6/58 and observe E[X] = 36 / 58. Generate, export, import valid JSON, then reject a duplicate-main-number record.

Native demonstration:

    drawlab moments --profile lab-5of36
    drawlab moments --profile lab-6of58
    drawlab generate --profile lab-5of36 --boards 2 > sample.json
    drawlab verify sample.json

Exact rational moments come from the Rust mathematical core and are also displayed by the WASM browser adapter. No probability approximation is used in the moment calculation. The mathematical domain is independent uniform k-subsets sampled without replacement, not physical draw equipment.

## Research boundaries

The model uses rejection-sampled unbiased bounded integers and a partial Fisher–Yates shuffle; under the uniform entropy model every k-subset occurs with probability 1/C(n,k). Expectations and variances derive from the hypergeometric model, with independent exhaustive small-domain checks. These are mathematical guarantees conditional on stated assumptions, not empirical certificates for live draws.

Research guidance:
- NIST SP 800-90B: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/90/b/final (entropy source design/validation and health testing).
- NIST SP 800-22 Rev. 1a: https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/22/r1/upd1/final (statistical tests cannot certify generator suitability).

DrawLab does not implement or claim NIST validation, external security audit, operator-rule conformance, authenticated physical randomness or empirical independence of official draws. Neither a valid JSON record nor a checksum proves randomness or origin. Extra/bonus events are excluded from moment analysis.
